1. What we collect

We only collect what we need to fulfil your order and answer your questions.

  • Order details — your name, phone number, delivery address, and any notes you add to an order.
  • Order history — the items you bought, the amount paid, and the status of the order.
  • Messages you send us — when you contact us through WhatsApp, Facebook, Line, Instagram, or any other channel listed on our site.
  • Basic technical data — your browser sends standard information such as IP address and browser type to our hosting provider as part of serving the page.

Payment card details

We never see or store your card number. Card payments are processed directly by Stripe, an independent payment provider. Your card details are sent from your browser to Stripe and never pass through our systems. We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, along with the amount and a reference number.

2. Why we use it

  • To process, confirm, pack, and ship your order.
  • To contact you about your order — confirmation, delivery updates, or a problem with an item.
  • To answer questions and provide support.
  • To keep accounting and tax records as required by law.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent orders.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary with the service providers that make the shop work:

  • Stripe — to take payment. Stripe acts as an independent data controller for payment data under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud) — hosting, database, and file storage for the website and order records.
  • Google reCAPTCHA — to confirm that requests to our checkout come from a real visitor and not an automated script. Google receives your IP address, browser information, and how you interact with the page, and uses it under its own privacy policy. We use this only to protect the shop from abuse; it does not affect what you are shown or charged.
  • Delivery and postal partners — your name, address, and phone number, so your parcel can be delivered.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, for example in response to a lawful request from an authority.

4. Where your data is held

Our shop is operated from Singapore and Thailand, and our hosting is provided by Google Cloud. This means your data may be stored or processed outside Singapore. Where data is transferred abroad, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil the order and to satisfy accounting, tax, and legal obligations. Enquiries and messages that do not lead to an order are deleted once they are no longer needed. When data is no longer required for any legal or business purpose, we delete or anonymise it.

6. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or third-party tracking pixels. Everything listed below is strictly necessary for the shop to function, which is why you are shown a notice rather than asked for consent.

We use your browser's local storage for:

  • a random identifier for your shopping cart, so its contents survive between pages;
  • your language choice (English or Thai);
  • a note that you have seen our cookie notice, so it is not shown again.

In addition, our service providers set their own cookies:

  • Google — for sign-in sessions if you create an account, and for reCAPTCHA, which protects the checkout from automated abuse.
  • Stripe — on the checkout step, for fraud prevention and to process your payment securely.

You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will empty your cart and reset your language choice.

7. Your rights under the PDPA

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and be told how it has been used.
  • Correct any personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Withdraw consent to our use of your personal data at any time. Note that if you withdraw consent we may no longer be able to complete an order that is already in progress.

To make any of these requests, contact us at the address above. We will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and access to order data is restricted to authorised staff accounts. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access.

9. Children

Our shop is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.

11. Contact us

For any question about this policy, or to exercise the rights described above, write to us at yingyingyingamulet@gmail.com, or reach us through any of the channels listed on our home page.
