Shipping

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Amulets ship from Thailand 🇹🇭 to Singapore 🇸🇬. Mailing is charged at a flat S$6 per order. Items are reserved and dispatched only after payment has been received.

1. Where we ship

We ship to Singapore and Malaysia as standard, and we can arrange delivery to most other countries on request. If your country is not listed at checkout, message us before ordering and we will confirm whether we can deliver and what it will cost.

2. Cost

  • S$6 flat mailing charge per order to Singapore.
  • Multiple items bought together ship as one parcel and are charged once.
  • Destinations outside Singapore and Malaysia are quoted individually before you pay.

3. Dispatch time

Orders are prepared once payment clears. Because our pieces are held in Thailand, an order typically leaves within 2 to 5 business days of payment. Items that need casing work are dispatched once that work is complete — we will give you a timeframe when you order.

4. Delivery time

After dispatch, allow approximately:

  • Singapore — 5 to 10 business days
  • Malaysia — 7 to 14 business days
  • Elsewhere — quoted at the time of order

These are estimates, not guarantees. Postal services, public holidays in Thailand and the destination country, and customs inspection can all add time.

5. Tracking

Where a tracked service is used we will send you the tracking number once the parcel is dispatched. If you have not received tracking within a week of paying, please contact us.

6. Customs and import duties

Parcels crossing a border may attract import duty, GST, or handling fees in the destination country. Any such charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the price you pay us. We declare parcels accurately as required by law.

7. Delivery address

Please check your address carefully before paying. We ship to the address given on the order, and we cannot be held responsible for a parcel delivered correctly to an address you supplied incorrectly. If you spot a mistake, tell us immediately — we can usually correct it if the parcel has not yet been dispatched.

8. Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, or does not arrive within a reasonable time after the estimates above, contact us. Keep all packaging. See our Refunds & Returns policy for how claims are handled.

9. Contact

Questions about a delivery? Write to yingyingyingamulet@gmail.com or message us through any channel on our home page, quoting your order reference.
